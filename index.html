IDAF Chemie & Geschichte

This project explores chemistry and history together through the development of an energy cell experiment and its link to the evolution of electric vehicles.

About Our Group and Goal

We are a group consisting of Fabian, Nicky, Levin, and Marin.

For our IDAF (Interdisziplinarische Allgemeine Facharbeit) with the theme chemistry and history, we first investigated the chemical reaction in an energy cell we created using steel wool, dense saltwater, and nails while passing energy through it.

This led us to the topic of historical electric cars and what has changed over time culturally, historically, and chemically.
